GTAVI Guide Hub is an independent fan-made guide site. The goal is to help players prepare for Grand Theft Auto VI with clear source labels, spoiler-safe structure, and practical setup guidance.

Source Standards

Confirmed facts come from Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, official console store pages, or information verified after launch. If a detail is not official, it is not presented as a confirmed fact.

LabelWhat It MeansExample Use

Corrections

If a page is outdated or unclear, send a correction note to Johnny.luo0103@outlook.com. Include the page URL, the sentence that needs correction, and the source you believe should be used.

Affiliate And Sponsor Separation

Some product links may be affiliate links. Product suggestions are kept separate from official GTA VI facts, and no product is presented as required for GTA VI unless an official source confirms it.

Spoiler Policy

Before launch, this site avoids leaks and story claims that are not official. After launch, story-sensitive pages should place major spoilers below clear warnings so readers can use setup and checklist content safely.
