GTA 6 Beginner Guide

›Guide Home

This beginner page is prepared for launch-week updates. It is designed for players who want a simple first-hour path without major story spoilers.

First-Hour Priorities

  • Check display, audio, subtitle, controller, and accessibility settings.
  • Learn driving, combat, cover, phone, map, and wanted-level basics.
  • Confirm autosave/manual save behavior before exploring heavily.
  • Avoid spending heavily until early money systems are understood.

Spoiler-Safe Guide Style

Mission pages should offer spoiler-free objective summaries first, then hide detailed story consequences behind clear spoiler warnings.
