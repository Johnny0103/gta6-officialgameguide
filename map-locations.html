GTA 6 Map Locations Guide

This page is the starting structure for a GTA 6 map guide. It focuses on Vice City, Leonida regions, and the location categories players are most likely to search for after launch.

Location Categories To Track

  • Story mission starting points and safe spoiler labels.
  • Collectibles, activities, side missions, shops, and services.
  • Vehicle spawn points, weapon shops, properties, and business locations.
  • Region pages for Vice City and wider Leonida areas.

Interactive Map Requirements

The full guide should eventually support marker filters, completion states, screenshots, search, region grouping, and notes for missable or time-sensitive content.
