Vehicle FAQ

What makes a vehicle Confirmed?

A vehicle or category is Confirmed only when it is visible or named in official Rockstar GTA VI material. Edition-bonus names are confirmed; their stats and general unlock paths still need verification.

What will improve this page after launch?

The highest-value updates will be spawn locations, garage capacity, customization options, race classes, prices, rare variants, and whether any vehicle task counts toward 100% completion.
