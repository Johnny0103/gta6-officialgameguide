About This GTA 6 Guide

›Guide Home

This website is an unofficial fan-made guide for Grand Theft Auto VI. It is designed to help players track completion progress, prepare for launch, and quickly find structured information about missions, activities, collectibles, vehicles, weapons, and map locations.

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Editorial Standards

  • Officially confirmed details are separated from expected or pending guide topics.
  • Pre-release content is labeled carefully to avoid presenting speculation as fact.
  • After launch, checklist items should be updated from direct gameplay verification.
  • Corrections and contact requests can be sent through the contact page.
