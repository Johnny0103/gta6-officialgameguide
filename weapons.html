Weapon FAQ

What is safe to trust before launch?

Trust broad categories and official-media evidence. Do not trust leaked weapon lists, fake stats, or claimed unlock levels unless Rockstar or Take-Two confirms them.

What gets updated first?

After launch, the first updates should be weapon shop locations, prices, ammo limits, attachment slots, early-game recommendations, and mission or challenge requirements.
